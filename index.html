FAQ

Questions, answered

I'm not technical. Will I be able to follow it?

Yes. It was written for owners, office managers and freelancers, not IT staff. Every page is a short explanation followed by a checklist, and most steps take minutes in apps you already use.

How long does it take to put into practice?

The guide includes a 30-day plan that takes roughly 2–3 hours per week. You can do the most important steps, like turning on two-factor authentication for email and banking, in a single afternoon.

Do I need to buy expensive security software?

No. Most steps use free settings already built into Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, your bank and your devices. The only recommended purchases are a password manager and, optionally, a couple of hardware security keys.

Is it OK for my team to use ChatGPT or other AI tools?

Yes, with rules. The Blueprint shows you how to pick approved tools, adjust their privacy settings, and decide what data is safe to share, so your team gets the productivity benefits without leaking client information.

Does this work outside the US?

The security habits apply anywhere. A few references, such as where to report fraud and breach-notification rules, are US-specific, so international readers should swap in their local equivalents.
